#  Food Truck Business Analysis

##  Food Truck Sales Performance Dashboard — FY 2024

A complete **Power BI business analytics project** for understanding food-truck sales performance, menu performance, location profitability, customer payment behavior, and demand patterns.

The project combines **data analysis, dashboard design, business storytelling, and actionable recommendations** in a three-page Power BI dashboard.

---

##  Project Objective

The objective of this project is to turn food-truck transaction data into practical business insights that can support:

- Revenue and profit optimization
- Menu and product decisions
- Location strategy
- Staffing and inventory planning
- Payment and checkout optimization
- Customer demand analysis

---

##  Dashboard Structure

- Key Customer & Store Insights

##  Key Business Results

| KPI | FY 2024 |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | ₹147K |
| Total Profit | ₹85K |
| Total Orders | 10K |
| Average Order Value | ₹15 |
| Profit Margin | 58% |

### Major Findings

- **Chinese** is the largest revenue category at **₹58.11K (39.64%)**.
- **Rice** contributes **₹44.46K (30.27%)**.
- **Chicken Biryani** is the top revenue item at **₹23K** and the top profit contributor at **₹12K**.
- **Samosa (2 Pieces)** is the highest-volume item at approximately **2.6K units**.
- **Times Square** leads on the reported average order value at **₹32K** and profit at **₹18K**.
- **Coney Island Boardwalk** is the weakest reported location at **₹14K AOV** and **₹8K profit**.
- **Saturday and Sunday** each generate approximately **1.7K orders**, creating a clear weekend demand peak.
- **Credit Card** is the most-used payment method at **34.68%** of orders.
- Cashless methods together account for approximately **90%** of transactions.

##  Business Recommendations

### Menu Strategy

- Protect and promote the strongest products, especially Chicken Biryani and Chicken Fried Rice.
- Use Samosa and other high-margin snacks as combo or add-on products.
- Review weaker menu items through limited-time promotions before simplifying the menu.

### Location Strategy

- Study why Times Square and Brooklyn Bridge Park outperform other locations.
- Compare foot traffic, customer mix, operating hours, events, and menu mix.
- Test pricing and menu strategies at lower-performing locations.

### Operations

- Increase staffing and inventory preparation before the weekend demand surge.
- Maintain reliable contactless payment infrastructure.
- Track revenue and orders together to distinguish higher footfall from higher basket value.
